CHRONICLES OF POWER

The Presidential Timeline

1899

First Philippine Republic

Emilio Aguinaldo is inaugurated in Malolos, marking the birth of the first democracy in Asia.

Republic Era
1935

Commonwealth Government

Manuel L. Quezon becomes the first President to occupy Malacañan Palace as the official residence.

Commonwealth Era
1943

Second Republic

The Japanese-sponsored republic under Jose P. Laurel during WWII.

Republic Era
1946

Third Republic

The Philippines gains full sovereignty from the United States with Manuel Roxas as President.

Republic Era
1965 - 1986

The Fourth Republic

The era of the New Society and Martial Law under Ferdinand Marcos Sr.

Republic Era
1986

People Power Revolution

A historic non-violent revolution restores democracy and installs Corazon Aquino.

Modern Era
1987 - 2010

Fifth Republic Foundation

Presidencies of Ramos, Estrada, and Arroyo focus on economic recovery and reform.

Modern Era
2010 - 2022

Contemporary Era

The administrations of Benigno Aquino III and Rodrigo Duterte navigate globalization and social shifts.

Modern Era
2022 - Present

Current Administration

Ferdinand Marcos Jr. assumes office, focusing on national unity and post-pandemic recovery.

Modern Era
