OUR MISSION

Safeguarding Philippine Presidential History

The Malacañang Archive serves as the primary custodian for the presidential records, personal papers, and historical artifacts of the Presidents of the Philippines. We ensure that the narrative of national leadership remains accessible for future generations of Filipinos.
